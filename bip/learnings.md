# What the posts have taught us

The desk session keeps this. Hypothesis first, then the number, then the call. A
finding needs at least five posts behind it; below that, write "anecdote".

## Starting assumptions, not yet tested

Taken from published posting studies, all vendor analyses with their own methods.

- Weekday mornings in the audience's timezone beat afternoons and weekends for tech.
  Slots are 08:00 ET, which is 23:00 in Sydney so the user can reply live.
- Threads of 4 to 7 posts get the highest engagement per post. The launch is three
  threads of 7, 8 and 8 rather than one of 21.
- Most engagement arrives in the first hour, so replies in that hour matter most.
